## Next: Live TV guide, step by step

Design concept and plan: [LIVE_TV_DESIGN.md](./LIVE_TV_DESIGN.md) (based on Nuvio + IPTV for Android TV, adapted to tvOS).

0. ✅ **Foundation**: design tokens, glass panel, activity pill for background work. Channels load one request after another (no more HTTP 404 right after sign-in).
1. ✅ **Guide grid**: groups · channels · timeline with now line (TiviMate style). Two-hour window in half-hour steps, Right/Left move through the programmes; Left on now goes to the channel, then to the groups (fast presses and swipes stop on the way); back from the player on the channel watched last; programmes come from the stored guide for visible rows only, short EPG for channels the guide doesn't cover. Timeline logic has Linux tests in CI.
2. ✅ **Info panel**: glass panel above the guide with the channel line (LIVE / CATCH-UP tags), the programme under the cursor, times with time left, progress and description; follows focus after a short rest, so scrolling stays smooth.
3. ✅ **Preview window**: OK plays a channel small in the info panel, OK again makes the same picture full screen (one player, one stream: fine for accounts with a single connection). Up/Down switch channels in full screen, Menu goes back to the guide on the channel playing. Leaving Live TV stops playback.
4. ✅ **Player info bar & overlay**
   - ✅ 4a: info bar in full screen (channel line, what's on with time left and progress, clock, up next) and the channel row of recently watched channels. Full screen is an app-level screen like the film player: no sidebar, no tab bar, one picture layer shared with the preview. Remote: Up/Down switch channels, Right shows the bar, OK opens the channel row, Left/Menu go back to the guide on the channel playing. Entering the guide from the groups or the header lands on the channel.
   - ✅ 4b: Left in full screen opens the guide over the picture (TiviMate's overlay mode): the group's channels with what's on, the highlighted channel's shows and the details; Left again the groups. OK plays a channel (its group becomes the one zapped through), Menu closes, it closes itself after 15 s. Swipes scroll by how far and fast the finger moves; only the rows on screen are drawn. Right opens the highlighted channel's schedule, Right again its dates (step 6).
5. ✅ **Quick menu & Settings → Live TV**: the gear in the header opens the guide's quick menu over the guide; Settings → Live TV in the app's settings style. Per profile, every change shows at once. Guide: info panel, description, preview window (off: OK goes straight to full screen), smaller top, channel counts, hide separator rows (off by default), clock (system, 24-hour, 12-hour). Playback: open on last group, info bar when switching, reverse channel up/down. Account and groups stay in Live TV's header for now; Integration comes with the Home row (step 7).
6. ✅ **Catch-up / restart** for channels with archive: in the guide OK on a past show plays it from the archive (↺ marks the shows that can), long press OK on the show on now offers *Watch from the beginning*. In the full-screen overlay Right opens the channel's schedule (days with headings, lands on the show on now), Right again the dates; OK on a past show plays it, on the show on now goes live. In a replay ◀/▶ jump 10 s back / 30 s ahead, Up/Down go back to live channels. Play/Pause pauses live and replays; pressed again in a replay it asks: *Continue watching* or *Back to live*. Catch-up links follow the Android app (several URL forms tried in turn).
7. ✅ **Reminders & Home row**
   - ✅ 7a: reminders as on Android. OK (or long press) on a show to come in the guide or the overlay schedule: *Remind me* / *Cancel reminder*; a bell marks it. A minute before the start a prompt comes up over any screen (also over a film) with *Watch* (Live TV full screen on the channel) and *Dismiss*; gone after two minutes. Per profile; listed in Settings → Live TV / VOD → Reminders, select one to remove it.
   - ✅ 7b: Live TV row on Home, below Continue Watching: favorite channels (recently watched while there are none, or by choice) with what's on, progress and time left. A card in focus shows the channel, the show with times, what's next and the description above the rows, on a dark background. OK plays full screen, Up/Down go through the row's channels. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Integration: on / off and the channels.
   - Profiles: Live TV (row, reminders, options, guide) and the Jellyfin / SMB libraries reload when switching profiles; new profiles no longer get a copy of the Jellyfin servers.
8. ✅ **Guide matching**
   - ✅ 8a: channels without a working guide id are matched by name, as on Android ("DE: Sky Cinema HD" = "Sky Cinema": prefixes, HD / FHD / 4K and brackets don't count). Chosen by hand first, then the guide id, then the name; only guide channels with programmes. The guide is read again once after the update.
   - ✅ 8b: *Assign EPG* (long press on a channel in the guide): suggestions with the closest names, search over all guide channels, *Automatic* or *No guide*. Per profile; the guide is read again after a change.

9. ✅ **Extra XMLTV guides** (as Android's EPG sources): Settings → Live TV / VOD → Guide Sources. Add a guide by link (`.xml` or `.xml.gz`, unpacked on the device in steps, the link kept in the Keychain), turn it on or off, move its listings by −12 to +12 hours in half hours. The provider's own guide can be turned off. All guides are read into one guide; each channel takes, as on Android, a choice by hand first, then real listings over placeholders ("No information" …), an id match over a name, the one going furthest ahead, the added guide before the provider's. Each guide shows how many programmes and channels it brought, or why it failed; one guide failing doesn't stop the others. *Assign EPG* lists the channels of all guides. Per profile. After saving a guide, *Update the guide now?* (also without Live TV open). The downloaded files are kept: a choice in *Assign EPG* is written at once when its programmes are stored already (or *No guide*), otherwise the kept files are read again without downloading.
10. ✅ **Channel list editing** (as Android's "Edit channel" and "Hide & arrange"), in three parts:
   - ✅ 10a: *Rename Channel* and *Rename Group* (long press in the guide or the groups); empty goes back to the automatic name. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Channel List: *Name Cleanup* takes words off the start or end of channel names, as on Android ("USA: ESPN HD" → "ESPN", never in the middle, never the whole name; a word with only a space after it counts when 5+ channels start with it), *Add Common Prefixes & Suffixes*, *Also Clean Group Names*, resets. Guide matching and Assign EPG keep the provider's names; search finds the new ones. The guide relabels in place, focus stays. Per profile. The settings sheet for a guide source now looks like the other settings sheets (Jellyfin, SMB).
   - ✅ 10b: *Reorder Channels* (long press a channel; every group but Search and Recently watched, Favorites included) and *Reorder Groups* (long press a group; Search, Favorites, Recent and All stay on top). OK picks one up (framed), move freely to the new place (fast through long lists, unlike Android's step by step), OK puts it there; Back saves. As on Android, a channel or group the provider adds later goes right behind the one above it, not to the end. Resets in Settings → Live TV / VOD → Channel List. Going back to the provider's name no longer counts as a rename.
   - ✅ 10c: your own groups, as Android's custom groups. Long press a channel → *Add to Group…*: one of yours, or a new one made with it. Channels go in by reference (they stay in their own group, also ones from hidden groups) and get their guide. Your groups sit above the provider's and *Reorder Groups* arranges them together. In one of your groups: *Remove from This Group* and *Reorder Channels*. Long press a group: *Rename Group*, *Delete Group* (asks first), *New Group*. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Channel List → *Delete My Groups*. Per profile. A channel taken out of a list (your group, Favorites) leaves animated, focus moves on to the next one.

11. ✅ **Parental controls** (as Android's): Settings → Live TV / VOD → Parental Controls → *Set a PIN* (4 to 8 digits, in the Keychain, per profile), *Change PIN*, *Lock Adult Content Automatically* (groups named Adult, XXX, 18+ …, by the shown and the provider's name; turning it off asks for the PIN), *Turn Off Parental Controls* (asks for the PIN, locked groups are remembered). Long press a group → *Lock with PIN* / *Remove Parental Lock* (asks for the PIN), also for your own groups. A locked group shows a lock instead of its count and asks for the PIN when opened; it stays open until the app is closed. After 5 wrong PINs in a row the next try waits 30 s, doubling up to 15 minutes (kept over app restarts). Its channels stay out of All Channels, search, favorites, recently watched, your groups, the Home row and the full-screen overlay until then; the Home row never saves channels of a group only unlocked for the session. Also: Live TV keeps its channel list between visits (opens at once instead of loading again; a new list in the background when the kept one is older than 12 hours, built again after a profile switch), and Left from the channel to the groups no longer needs a second swipe when the finger rested on the remote.
12. ✅ **VOD** (Android's provider movies and series), in three parts. The provider's copy of a title comes in its stream list; the page and Home row are for browsing what the provider has.
   - ✅ 12a: the provider's movies and series are read once a day (20 s after the start, or when a title is opened; not while Live TV plays full screen) into SQLite per profile; a failed read is tried again after an hour. Every title's stream list gets a *VOD* group, last: matched by the provider's TMDB id, otherwise by name (provider tags like "EN - " learned from the catalog, brackets, years and quality words off) with the year within one; episodes through `get_series_info` (one season only: the episode number is enough). Best quality first, at most 6. Auto-play picks it only when nothing else plays. Categories locked with the parental PIN, or adult ones with the automatic lock, never show up. Settings → Live TV / VOD → VOD: *VOD in Streams*, *Update Movies & Series Now* with counts. Also: a quick left swipe on the channel that the guide's swipe recognizer caught now goes on to the groups.
   - ✅ 12b: pages in the sidebar and rows on Home. *VOD* page (under Live TV, with a provider login): Movies / Series, a row per provider category in its order, 30 titles at a time, more while scrolling; categories locked with the parental PIN or adult ones (automatic lock) left out. *Jellyfin* page (with a server): a row per library. Home: a *VOD* row (what the provider added last, movies and series) next to the existing Jellyfin rows. Opening a provider title: Nuvio's own details when it matches (the provider's TMDB id → IMDb with a TMDB key, otherwise a Cinemeta search by name and year; at most a few seconds, "Opening…" meanwhile), the provider's copy then in its stream list; otherwise Details from the provider (`get_vod_info` / `get_series_info`: plot, pictures, seasons and episodes) playing its own file. Settings → Appearance → Sidebar: *VOD*, *Jellyfin*; Home Rows: *VOD*, *Jellyfin* (pages and rows only; streams stay). The index keeps pictures, dates and category order now (read again once after the update). Also: entering the guide from the groups, Left goes straight back. Called *VOD* everywhere now (Settings → *Live TV / VOD*). An empty VOD or Jellyfin page has a *Settings* button (focus no longer gets stuck there). Left from a programme onto the channel no longer gets lost on a short swipe. From what's on now onto the channel, the next swipe goes straight on to the groups (no pause; only the same swipe stops on the channel).
   - ✅ 12c: VOD in search, categories, versions, duplicates. *Search* (every search style): the provider's titles the add-ons don't find come at the end of the results (movies and series, at most 40, by name). *Categories* (Settings → Live TV / VOD → VOD → Categories, or *Categories* on the VOD page): show or hide each category, lock it with the parental PIN (removing a lock asks for it); hidden ones are out of the page, Home, search and the stream lists, as on Android. *Versions*: a provider title's stream list offers every copy (other qualities and categories, up to 6, this one first). *Merge Duplicates* (on by default): the same TMDB id, or the same name and year, shows up once in the page rows, the Home row and search. Settings: *VOD in Search*, *Merge Duplicates*. *Languages* (Settings → Live TV / VOD → VOD → Languages, or *Languages* on the VOD page): the language of each title is read from its tag ("DE - ", "4K-AR: ", "|TR| "), its category ("FR - ANIMATION", "Arabic Movies") or the script of the category name; pick the languages to show (none picked: all), once one is picked, titles with no language given are out too unless turned on, and a stream list with a version in a picked language drops the ones without; Scandinavian packs ("4K-SC - ", "NORDIC FILM") are one group, *Nordic*; per profile, everywhere VOD shows. Tags that look like tags (capitals, a language or quality in them: "4K-NF - ") now come off names even when not learned, so more copies match and more titles open with Nuvio's details; versions list one of each quality before more of the same (up to 8). Loading shows how far it is, as the guide does: as Jellyfin's, how many movies or series are in so far ("Loading movies · 12,500"), then how many are saved. The index is read again once after the update.

13. 🚧 **Sources: several Xtream logins, M3U playlists, Stalker portals** (as Android's *Playlists & Guides*), in five parts. Every source is one entry in a list per profile (secrets in the Keychain), managed like Nuvio's Jellyfin servers. The guide mixes them into one list, as on Android: *All channels* covers every source and the group column has a heading per source that folds. The login you have now becomes the first source with the same channel ids, so favorites, names, order, groups and locks stay.
   - 🚧 13a: several Xtream logins. Settings → Live TV / VOD → *Sources*: add, edit, turn off, move, update and delete logins; each row shows its channels, last update or error. Channels, guides (each login's own guide), playback and catch-up per source; a source that fails leaves the others.
   - ⏭ 13b: M3U playlists (link, optional user agent): tvg-id, tvg-name, tvg-logo, group-title, tvg-chno, headers (#EXTVLCOPT, #EXTHTTP, url|User-Agent=), the guide linked in the playlist (x-tvg-url); movie and series entries left out, as on Android.
   - ⏭ 13c: VOD from every Xtream login: one catalogue, duplicates merged, versions show the source.
   - ⏭ 13d: catch-up for M3U (catchup, catchup-source, catchup-days: default, append, shift, flussonic, xc).
   - ⏭ 13e: Stalker / Ministra portals (portal URL and MAC address).
