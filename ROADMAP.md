## Next: Live TV guide, step by step

Design concept and plan: [LIVE_TV_DESIGN.md](./LIVE_TV_DESIGN.md) (based on Nuvio + IPTV for Android TV, adapted to tvOS).

0. ✅ **Foundation**: design tokens, glass panel, activity pill for background work. Channels load one request after another (no more HTTP 404 right after sign-in).
1. ✅ **Guide grid**: groups · channels · timeline with now line (TiviMate style). Two-hour window in half-hour steps, Right/Left move through the programmes; Left on now goes to the channel, then to the groups (fast presses and swipes stop on the way); back from the player on the channel watched last; programmes come from the stored guide for visible rows only, short EPG for channels the guide doesn't cover. Timeline logic has Linux tests in CI.
2. ✅ **Info panel**: glass panel above the guide with the channel line (LIVE / CATCH-UP tags), the programme under the cursor, times with time left, progress and description; follows focus after a short rest, so scrolling stays smooth.
3. ✅ **Preview window**: OK plays a channel small in the info panel, OK again makes the same picture full screen (one player, one stream: fine for accounts with a single connection). Up/Down switch channels in full screen, Menu goes back to the guide on the channel playing. Leaving Live TV stops playback.
4. ✅ **Player info bar & overlay**
   - ✅ 4a: info bar in full screen (channel line, what's on with time left and progress, clock, up next) and the channel row of recently watched channels. Full screen is an app-level screen like the film player: no sidebar, no tab bar, one picture layer shared with the preview. Remote: Up/Down switch channels, Right shows the bar, OK opens the channel row, Left/Menu go back to the guide on the channel playing. Entering the guide from the groups or the header lands on the channel.
   - ✅ 4b: Left in full screen opens the guide over the picture (TiviMate's overlay mode): the group's channels with what's on, the highlighted channel's shows and the details; Left again the groups. OK plays a channel (its group becomes the one zapped through), Menu closes, it closes itself after 15 s. Swipes scroll by how far and fast the finger moves; only the rows on screen are drawn. Right opens the highlighted channel's schedule, Right again its dates (step 6).
5. ✅ **Quick menu & Settings → Live TV**: the gear in the header opens the guide's quick menu over the guide; Settings → Live TV in the app's settings style. Per profile, every change shows at once. Guide: info panel, description, preview window (off: OK goes straight to full screen), smaller top, channel counts, hide separator rows (off by default), clock (system, 24-hour, 12-hour). Playback: open on last group, info bar when switching, reverse channel up/down. Account and groups stay in Live TV's header for now; Integration comes with the Home row (step 7).
6. ✅ **Catch-up / restart** for channels with archive: in the guide OK on a past show plays it from the archive (↺ marks the shows that can), long press OK on the show on now offers *Watch from the beginning*. In the full-screen overlay Right opens the channel's schedule (days with headings, lands on the show on now), Right again the dates; OK on a past show plays it, on the show on now goes live. In a replay ◀/▶ jump 10 s back / 30 s ahead, Up/Down go back to live channels. Play/Pause pauses live and replays; pressed again in a replay it asks: *Continue watching* or *Back to live*. Catch-up links follow the Android app (several URL forms tried in turn).
7. ✅ **Reminders & Home row**
   - ✅ 7a: reminders as on Android. OK (or long press) on a show to come in the guide or the overlay schedule: *Remind me* / *Cancel reminder*; a bell marks it. A minute before the start a prompt comes up over any screen (also over a film) with *Watch* (Live TV full screen on the channel) and *Dismiss*; gone after two minutes. Per profile; listed in Settings → Live TV / VOD → Reminders, select one to remove it.
   - ✅ 7b: Live TV row on Home, below Continue Watching: favorite channels (recently watched while there are none, or by choice) with what's on, progress and time left. A card in focus shows the channel, the show with times, what's next and the description above the rows, on a dark background. OK plays full screen, Up/Down go through the row's channels. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Integration: on / off and the channels.
   - Profiles: Live TV (row, reminders, options, guide) and the Jellyfin / SMB libraries reload when switching profiles; new profiles no longer get a copy of the Jellyfin servers.
8. ✅ **Guide matching**
   - ✅ 8a: channels without a working guide id are matched by name, as on Android ("DE: Sky Cinema HD" = "Sky Cinema": prefixes, HD / FHD / 4K and brackets don't count). Chosen by hand first, then the guide id, then the name; only guide channels with programmes. The guide is read again once after the update.
   - ✅ 8b: *Assign EPG* (long press on a channel in the guide): suggestions with the closest names, search over all guide channels, *Automatic* or *No guide*. Per profile; the guide is read again after a change.

9. ✅ **Extra XMLTV guides** (as Android's EPG sources): Settings → Live TV / VOD → Guide Sources. Add a guide by link (`.xml` or `.xml.gz`, unpacked on the device in steps, the link kept in the Keychain), turn it on or off, move its listings by −12 to +12 hours in half hours. The provider's own guide can be turned off. All guides are read into one guide; each channel takes, as on Android, a choice by hand first, then real listings over placeholders ("No information" …), an id match over a name, the one going furthest ahead, the added guide before the provider's. Each guide shows how many programmes and channels it brought, or why it failed; one guide failing doesn't stop the others. *Assign EPG* lists the channels of all guides. Per profile. After saving a guide, *Update the guide now?* (also without Live TV open). The downloaded files are kept: a choice in *Assign EPG* is written at once when its programmes are stored already (or *No guide*), otherwise the kept files are read again without downloading.
10. ✅ **Channel list editing** (as Android's "Edit channel" and "Hide & arrange"), in three parts:
   - ✅ 10a: *Rename Channel* and *Rename Group* (long press in the guide or the groups); empty goes back to the automatic name. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Channel List: *Name Cleanup* takes words off the start or end of channel names, as on Android ("USA: ESPN HD" → "ESPN", never in the middle, never the whole name; a word with only a space after it counts when 5+ channels start with it), *Add Common Prefixes & Suffixes*, *Also Clean Group Names*, resets. Guide matching and Assign EPG keep the provider's names; search finds the new ones. The guide relabels in place, focus stays. Per profile. The settings sheet for a guide source now looks like the other settings sheets (Jellyfin, SMB).
   - ✅ 10b: *Reorder Channels* (long press a channel; every group but Search and Recently watched, Favorites included) and *Reorder Groups* (long press a group; Search, Favorites, Recent and All stay on top). OK picks one up (framed), move freely to the new place (fast through long lists, unlike Android's step by step), OK puts it there; Back saves. As on Android, a channel or group the provider adds later goes right behind the one above it, not to the end. Resets in Settings → Live TV / VOD → Channel List. Going back to the provider's name no longer counts as a rename.
   - ✅ 10c: your own groups, as Android's custom groups. Long press a channel → *Add to Group…*: one of yours, or a new one made with it. Channels go in by reference (they stay in their own group, also ones from hidden groups) and get their guide. Your groups sit above the provider's and *Reorder Groups* arranges them together. In one of your groups: *Remove from This Group* and *Reorder Channels*. Long press a group: *Rename Group*, *Delete Group* (asks first), *New Group*. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Channel List → *Delete My Groups*. Per profile. A channel taken out of a list (your group, Favorites) leaves animated, focus moves on to the next one.

11. ✅ **Parental controls** (as Android's): Settings → Live TV / VOD → Parental Controls → *Set a PIN* (4 to 8 digits, in the Keychain, per profile), *Change PIN*, *Lock Adult Content Automatically* (groups named Adult, XXX, 18+ …, by the shown and the provider's name; turning it off asks for the PIN), *Turn Off Parental Controls* (asks for the PIN, locked groups are remembered). Long press a group → *Lock with PIN* / *Remove Parental Lock* (asks for the PIN), also for your own groups. A locked group shows a lock instead of its count and asks for the PIN when opened; it stays open until the app is closed. After 5 wrong PINs in a row the next try waits 30 s, doubling up to 15 minutes (kept over app restarts). Its channels stay out of All Channels, search, favorites, recently watched, your groups, the Home row and the full-screen overlay until then; the Home row never saves channels of a group only unlocked for the session. Also: Live TV keeps its channel list between visits (opens at once instead of loading again; a new list in the background when the kept one is older than 12 hours, built again after a profile switch), and Left from the channel to the groups no longer needs a second swipe when the finger rested on the remote.
12. ✅ **VOD** (Android's provider movies and series), in three parts. The provider's copy of a title comes in its stream list; the page and Home row are for browsing what the provider has.
   - ✅ 12a: the provider's movies and series are read once a day (20 s after the start, or when a title is opened; not while Live TV plays full screen) into SQLite per profile; a failed read is tried again after an hour. Every title's stream list gets a *VOD* group, last: matched by the provider's TMDB id, otherwise by name (provider tags like "EN - " learned from the catalog, brackets, years and quality words off) with the year within one; episodes through `get_series_info` (one season only: the episode number is enough). Best quality first, at most 6. Auto-play picks it only when nothing else plays. Categories locked with the parental PIN, or adult ones with the automatic lock, never show up. Settings → Live TV / VOD → VOD: *VOD in Streams*, *Update Movies & Series Now* with counts. Also: a quick left swipe on the channel that the guide's swipe recognizer caught now goes on to the groups.
   - ✅ 12b: pages in the sidebar and rows on Home. *VOD* page (under Live TV, with a provider login): Movies / Series, a row per provider category in its order, 30 titles at a time, more while scrolling; categories locked with the parental PIN or adult ones (automatic lock) left out. *Jellyfin* page (with a server): a row per library. Home: a *VOD* row (what the provider added last, movies and series) next to the existing Jellyfin rows. Opening a provider title: Nuvio's own details when it matches (the provider's TMDB id → IMDb with a TMDB key, otherwise a Cinemeta search by name and year; at most a few seconds, "Opening…" meanwhile), the provider's copy then in its stream list; otherwise Details from the provider (`get_vod_info` / `get_series_info`: plot, pictures, seasons and episodes) playing its own file. Settings → Appearance → Sidebar: *VOD*, *Jellyfin*; Home Rows: *VOD*, *Jellyfin* (pages and rows only; streams stay). The index keeps pictures, dates and category order now (read again once after the update). Also: entering the guide from the groups, Left goes straight back. Called *VOD* everywhere now (Settings → *Live TV / VOD*). An empty VOD or Jellyfin page has a *Settings* button (focus no longer gets stuck there). Left from a programme onto the channel no longer gets lost on a short swipe. From what's on now onto the channel, the next swipe goes straight on to the groups (no pause; only the same swipe stops on the channel).
   - ✅ 12c: VOD in search, categories, versions, duplicates. *Search* (every search style): the provider's titles the add-ons don't find come at the end of the results (movies and series, at most 40, by name). *Categories* (Settings → Live TV / VOD → VOD → Categories, or *Categories* on the VOD page): show or hide each category, lock it with the parental PIN (removing a lock asks for it); hidden ones are out of the page, Home, search and the stream lists, as on Android. *Versions*: a provider title's stream list offers every copy (other qualities and categories, up to 6, this one first). *Merge Duplicates* (on by default): the same TMDB id, or the same name and year, shows up once in the page rows, the Home row and search. Settings: *VOD in Search*, *Merge Duplicates*. *Languages* (Settings → Live TV / VOD → VOD → Languages, or *Languages* on the VOD page): the language of each title is read from its tag ("DE - ", "4K-AR: ", "|TR| "), its category ("FR - ANIMATION", "Arabic Movies") or the script of the category name; pick the languages to show (none picked: all), once one is picked, titles with no language given are out too unless turned on, and a stream list with a version in a picked language drops the ones without; Scandinavian packs ("4K-SC - ", "NORDIC FILM") are one group, *Nordic*; per profile, everywhere VOD shows. Tags that look like tags (capitals, a language or quality in them: "4K-NF - ") now come off names even when not learned, so more copies match and more titles open with Nuvio's details; versions list one of each quality before more of the same (up to 8). Loading shows how far it is, as the guide does: as Jellyfin's, how many movies or series are in so far ("Loading movies · 12,500"), then how many are saved. The index is read again once after the update.

13. 🧪 **Sources: several Xtream logins, M3U playlists, Stalker portals** (as Android's *Playlists & Guides*), in six parts. Every source is one entry in a list per profile (secrets in the Keychain), managed like Nuvio's Jellyfin servers. The guide mixes them into one list, as on Android: the group column has a heading per source that folds (the same arrow as Multiview's groups), each with its own *All channels* (also in the full-screen overlay's group column, with the same arrows and rows as Multiview's picker). The login you have now becomes the first source with the same channel ids, so favorites, names, order, groups and locks stay.
   - ✅ 13a: several Xtream logins (tested with two). Turning a source on or off, moving or renaming it keeps the other lists; the guide is read again from the kept files, not downloaded. A source's own guide is matched to its own channels only (guides you add go for every channel); the same guide link from two sources is read once. A source added, turned on or off changes only its part of the guide: one turned off just leaves (nothing is read), one added has its own guide read and the added guides from their kept files for its channels only; the other sources' programmes stay. A guide refresh that downloads the same files for the same channels reads nothing again (at most every 36 h, as the window moves on). Adding a login asks whether its movies and series are read for VOD (*Include Movies & Series*). Catch-up: replay links are followed to where the panel sends them before playing (some panels send to another server, the player got lost there), 20 s per form; when none plays, the message lists every form tried, its answer, the time zone and what the server sends. Assign EPG reads only the chosen guide channel from the kept file. Settings → Live TV / VOD → *Sources*: add, edit, turn off, move, update and delete logins; each row shows its channels, last update or error. Channels, guides (each login's own guide), playback and catch-up per source; a source that fails leaves the others.
   - ✅ 13b: M3U playlists (Settings → Sources → *Add M3U Playlist*; Live TV with no source has a *Set Up Sources* button that goes there, as Jellyfin is set up in Settings; link in the Keychain, optional user agent; read one after another with the logins, counted while it comes in): tvg-id, tvg-name, tvg-logo, group-title, tvg-chno, headers (#EXTVLCOPT, #EXTHTTP, url|User-Agent=), the guide linked in the playlist (x-tvg-url); movie and series entries left out, as on Android.
   - ✅ 13c: VOD from every Xtream login with *Include Movies & Series* on: one catalogue (each login read in turn, the others kept when one fails; a login removed or with VOD off leaves at once), duplicates merged across logins, versions show the login's name, playing and details ask the login the title is from. The index of the first login is kept. A list that comes back the same as last time (Update Now, the daily read) isn't saved again.
   - 🧪 13d: catch-up for M3U (no playlist with catch-up to test with yet), as Android's: catchup / catchup-type, catchup-source, catchup-days (tvg-rec, timeshift), catchup-correction, from the entry or the #EXTM3U line; default and append (templates with {utc}, {utcend}, {lutc}, {duration}, {offset}, {utc:Y-m-d H:M:S}, {Y}{m}{d}{H}{M}{S}), shift (utc / lutc), flussonic, xc; links that look like an Xtream server's get its forms tried in turn.
   - 🧪 13e: Stalker / Ministra portals, live channels and guide, as Android's: Settings → Sources → *Add Stalker Portal* (portal address, MAC address; a login and device ids only where the provider gives them; all in the Keychain). Sign-in checked on Save (handshake, profile, `do_auth` where the portal asks; a dropped token is renewed once; the MAC goes only to the portal's own server). Genres and channels (`get_all_channels`, else page by page); the link to play is asked from the portal when a channel starts (`create_link`). The portal's guide (`get_epg_info`, 3 days) is written as an XMLTV file and read like the others, for its own channels.
   - 🧪 13f: Stalker movies, series and catch-up, as Android's. *Include Movies & Series* on the portal's form too. Movies from the portal's movie list, series from its own series list (where there is one) and the series inside the movie list; categories with "Other" for titles in none. They join the VOD index (page, Home, search, stream lists, versions) as the Xtream logins' do; what plays a title is kept with it (the portal's id and command, never a link). Playing asks the portal for the link (`create_link`; a movie kept as one file is looked up first; several files: not played, as Android). Series: seasons and episodes from the portal (an older portal's episode numbers in the list, or seasons and episodes in the movie list). Catch-up: channels with the portal's archive (`tv_archive`, days) get ↺ and Watch from the beginning; a past show is found in that day's archive guide (`get_simple_data_table`, the show starting within five minutes of the guide's time) and played from the archive (`tv_archive` link). The VOD index gets a column for that (no reread). Also: the Live TV Home row, the VOD page and search show with any source, not only with an Xtream login that reads VOD.

## After that: from Nuvio + IPTV 1.2.0 for Android

The order agreed on (one pull request per step, as before):

14. ✅ **Time-shift**: pause and rewind live TV on every channel, as Android's *Pause and Rewind Live TV* (with its *Rewind Anytime*). Built on the player engine's own DVR window (AetherEngine keeps the channel playing from the same connection, no second one; at most a quarter of the free space): Play/Pause pauses, Play/Pause again asks *Continue watching* or *Back to live*; behind live, ◀ / ▶ skip 10 s back and 30 s on (on past live is live) and the info bar shows *TIME-SHIFT* and how far behind. Back to live jumps to the live edge without loading the channel again. Settings → Live TV / VOD → Playback: *Pause and Rewind Live TV* (on), *Keep Up To* 10 min, 30 min, 1 h or 2 h. Gone when the channel changes or Live TV closes. Not on the MPV fallback.
15. ✅ **Multiview**, in two parts:
   - ✅ 15a: up to four channels at once, as Android's. *Multiview* in Live TV's header (the channel playing in the first pane) or *Add to Multiview* in a channel's menu (with the channel playing, or into the next free pane). Eight layouts with little pictures (1; 2 side by side or stacked; 2 on top and 1 wide, 1 wide and 2 below, 1 big left and 2 right; 2 × 2, 1 wide and 3 below). Each pane its own player engine (one primary, the others secondary; the sound follows the pane in focus by volume, nothing loads again); panes never switch the TV's picture mode. Arrows move between panes as on Android (no wrap); OK on a pane opens the channels, as Android's: one list down the side away from the pane (your groups, then each source's heading that folds its own *All channels* and groups; with one source *All channels* on top; OK on a group opens its channels under it with number, logo, name and what's on now; locked groups stay out). The list is a native tvOS list with cell reuse (as Live TV's), so even 100,000 channels scroll smoothly, with tvOS's fast-scroll thumb on long swipes; Play/Pause grows the pane in focus to full screen and back (the others play on, silent; Left / Right go round them). The bar at the top (Up from a top pane): *Layout*, *Channels*, *Full Screen*, *Exit*, which pane is heard. Bar, names and edges fade after 5 s; Back closes a picker, then full screen, then the overlays, then leaves: the pane heard goes on full screen in the single player. The single player stops while Multiview is open (providers allow only so many connections).
   - ✅ 15b: a pane that fails or drops reconnects after a short wait; the second failure in a row moves it to a backup copy of the channel, as Android's *Backup channel* (the same name without country prefix, quality, fps or backup notes; other sources first, never another local station or a locked group; Settings → Live TV / VOD → *Backup Channel*, on by default); out of tries it shows the error. The channel picker's first row searches every channel: OK opens the tvOS keyboard straight away, as Settings' fields (also typing on the iPhone); up to 5,000 found, Menu ends the search. Each pane's name shows what's on now with how far it is (below the bar while it's up).
16. 🧪 **Calendar**, rebuilt as Android's (it replaces Nuvio's month grid in the Calendar tab), in parts:
   - 🧪 16a: the data as Android: the shows and films you follow (Nuvio's calendar follows), keep in the Library or watch (Continue Watching, watched episodes, Trakt); episodes from TVmaze (no key; one request at a time, kept on disk: ended shows a week, shows airing within a day and a half 3 h, others 20 h), films' theatrical, home and disc dates from TMDB (Nuvio's TMDB key; the Apple TV's region, else US), wide pictures from TMDB. 30 days back to 120 ahead; one card for several episodes dropped together; tags New, Series / Season premiere, Season / Series finale, In theaters, At home, On disc; East Coast shows at the West Coast feed's time on the West Coast and in Mountain time; episodes past the next one you'd watch keep their title, picture and text hidden. The List view: *Upcoming* and *Recently aired* (*Unwatched only*, in the quick menu), the days on the left with up to two wide cards in a row, each day under a bold name with its date, how many and a line across (today in the accent colour); the card in focus described on the right on a dark glass over its big picture (one box of the same size and place for every card, so nothing moves while going through them), which fills the screen: logo, episode (S02 · E05), day and time (*time not announced* when TVmaze has none, as for many streaming drops), network (its logo when TMDB has one), tags, ratings (the ones on in Settings → *Rating Providers & Order*, the app's own choice, in that order, each always in its place, "–" while unknown), year, genres and runtime, the episode's text, the show's or film's text (alone when the episode has none), cast with pictures (TMDB) and creators (the same metadata the title's page uses, asked for the card in focus only); OK opens the show on its episode or the film. Opens at once from what's on disk, asks again after 30 minutes or on *Refresh*. Faster than Android: one TVmaze request per show (seasons and episodes embedded), lookups read the show's id from the redirect (as Android); after the first load only shows TVmaze lists as changed in the last week are asked again (*Refresh* too), so a refresh takes one request plus the changed shows; a first load starts from TVmaze's schedule of coming episodes worldwide (one request, kept 12 h): the cards of shows on air show up in seconds and need no lookup, then each show is asked, those on air first; cards appear as shows come in; TMDB's pictures, network logos (CBS, Netflix… in place of the name, all white as in the Apple TV app: their shape, or only their lettering when they sit on a box or dark disc like TF1 or ABC) and the shows' own logos (in place of the title, in your language, else English, in colour); every logo cut to what's drawn and given the same area, wide or tall, are asked side by side with TVmaze. TVmaze's placeholder episode names (TBA) are left out. Settings → *Calendar* (its own place: the calendar is for everything you follow, not only Live TV): *Time Zone* (the Apple TV's, or one from a list; days and air times in that zone), *Hide Spoilers* (on), *Country for Film Dates* (the Apple TV's region, or one from a list). Long lists (time zone, country, languages) open as a list to pick from, as Nuvio's *Preferred Audio*.
   - 🧪 16b: List / Week / Month on the right of the header (kept per profile), the same buttons as the tabs. ‹ Week / Month › right under them, the card in focus below. Week: a column per day (the week starts as the Apple TV's region has it, today tinted) with small cards; ‹ Week, This week, Week ›. Month: a grid of six weeks, each day with its first card's picture darkened, the day and the first title (+N), today framed, days of other months faded; the chosen day's cards above the grid, OK on a day goes up to them; ‹ Month, This month, Month ›. The big picture and details follow the card or day in focus.
   - 🧪 16c: one header row as Android's (tabs left; for your own calendar a List / Week / Month switch and Refresh right; in Week and Month Upcoming and Recently aired are one tab, *My calendar*). The calendar is ready before it's opened: from the disk 3 s after the app starts, fresh after 40 s, then every 3 h, never while something plays. A gear after Refresh opens the calendar's quick menu (as the guide's): *Upcoming: Only On My TV*, *Recently Aired: Unwatched Only*, *Airing Today: Leave Out News & Talk*; the calendar changes as you go. Tabs: *Upcoming*, *Recently aired*, *Airing today* (TVmaze's schedule of today in the calendar's country, TV and that country's streaming, kept 2 h; news, talk, sports and panel shows left out; a show's episodes of today in a row on one card (E01–E04), on TV at the first one's time; filters All, Prime time 19–22 h, Streaming, Following; as Android it lists everything new on TV today, not only your shows; TV in your country always, streaming from elsewhere only in your country's language and English (Settings → Calendar → *Airing Today: Streaming Languages*, a list that opens: the country's language with English or alone, all, or another language alone or with English; the country's language is the country's own, not the Apple TV's); TV by time first, then streaming; TMDB's pictures and logos asked for its shows a dozen at a time), *Following* (each title with the bell: its next episode, else its last, else *Nothing announced yet* / *Waiting to come out*), *Hidden* (when there are any; OK shows a title again). The bell on show and film pages (next to AniList / MAL) and on every card (hold OK), which also offers *Hide this show from the Calendar*; it rings in Nuvio's calendar follows. The Month shows each day's first show by its logo on a calm tile, with +N.
   - 🧪 16d: *On my TV*, as Android's: the calendar's episodes (and Airing today's) found in your Live TV guide, from three hours ago to as far as the guide goes, read once in the background after each load, a new guide or new channels: the same show (its title, or the title followed by ": …", " - …", " (…)"), from 75 minutes before its air time to 200 after; best first the channel named for the network, your favourites, HD, the closest time. The card says the channel and time; the details say *On your TV: channel · 20:15–21:10 (+N channels)*; hold OK: *Watch live on …* (from 10 minutes before until it ends) or *Remind me* (Live TV's reminders). Upcoming gets an *On my TV* filter (in the quick menu). Not checked yet: the guide's episode numbers against reruns (the stored guide keeps none).
   - 🧪 16e: the rest of Android's Calendar settings: *Opens In* (List, Week, Month), *OK on an Episode in Your Guide* (*Live TV options*: watch live, remind me, or the page, as Android's; or *Show page*), *Airing Today: Leave Out News & Talk* (on). Android's Home rows (new-episode cards, the Upcoming row) are left out: Nuvio's Home already has its own *Today* row from its calendar. Sports games come after step 17.
17. 🚧 **Sports hub**, in steps: live scores and game info, standings, league channels, teams you follow, score and full-time alerts, games on the channels that show them, sports in the calendar.

## Noted for later

- 💡 **Recordings** (Android 1.2.0: until stopped, until the show ends, until a set time, series): tvOS doesn't record in the background or in standby, so at most while the app is open. The player engine can record the channel playing over its own connection (`startRecording`), which makes that part simpler.
- 💡 **Sync across devices** (Android's setup sync): only useful with several Apple TVs.
- Not needed on Apple TV: adding sources from the phone by QR code (the iPhone / iPad keyboard already types into every field).

- 💡 **German translations** for all texts (Live TV, account sync, calendar, Usenet, player, ...). Prepared, not applied: English first so the app works for everyone.
